BIZNORYX account deletion

Sign in and open Account to request deletion with your current password. Transfer last-owner workspaces to an active member or confirm their permanent closure. Sessions are revoked immediately.

Closed workspaces, uploaded files and personal account details are removed after billing and processing cleanup. Business records in shared workspaces remain with those businesses, with a non-identifying actor reference preserving their history. A limited deletion status record is retained. Backups and third-party retention are covered by our privacy policy.

Apple and Google subscriptions must be cancelled in the store account. Account deletion does not require waiting for a subscription to end.
