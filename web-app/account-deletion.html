Account deletion requests

BIZNORYX currently handles account and business-data deletion through support. You can reach the public support channel without signing in. Opening this page does not itself delete an account or submit a request.

Request removal

  1. Open BIZNORYX live support and start a conversation using the 3CX support bubble.
  2. Say that you want to delete your BIZNORYX account or specific business information. Provide the account email and identify the relevant business workspace.
  3. Explain whether you are asking to remove your personal account, particular records or an entire business workspace. Let support know if other members use that workspace.
  4. Support must verify your identity and authority, confirm the scope and explain any information that needs to be retained. Ask for confirmation when the request has been completed.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, access tokens, complete card details or identity documents in your initial chat message. If support is unavailable, an unanswered message is not confirmation of completed deletion.

Information covered by your request

A personal account request concerns your account profile, authentication and access records. A business-data request may concern uploaded files, manual entries, saved drafts, reports and associated workspace information. The scope depends on your authority and the confirmed request; a personal account closure must not silently remove another person's business records.

Shared business history may remain with a business that continues operating. Billing, security, dispute and legal records can have separate retention requirements. Infrastructure backups, logs, support conversations and email-delivery records have their own configured expiry schedules; specific periods have not been confirmed for this publication. Support must explain applicable exceptions and timing. Copies already exported to another device or recipient are outside this removal process.

Subscriptions are separate

A deletion request does not itself cancel recurring billing. Use the website's subscription controls or ask billing support for assistance. Mention any active subscription when requesting closure. You can raise a deletion request before resolving a billing issue.

Privacy details

To request removal of uploaded datasets or other specific business information while keeping your account, see BIZNORYX data deletion requests.

See the retention section and privacy contact section of the BIZNORYX Privacy Policy for more information.
