BIZNORYX Data Deletion Requests

You can request removal of specific uploaded datasets or other business information without deleting your account. Requests use the public BIZNORYX support channel; there is no self-service dataset-deletion button in the current dashboard.

Contact BIZNORYX support to request data deletion

Opening this page does not submit a request or delete any information. Account closure is a separate request: see account deletion requests.

How to request data deletion

  1. Open the support link above and start a conversation using the 3CX support bubble. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in to reach the Help page.
  2. Say: "I want to delete specific BIZNORYX business data and keep my account." Provide the account email and business workspace name.
  3. Identify the information concerned: for example, the uploaded filename, data series and reporting month, or the dates of manual entries. Specify whether you want selected records or all business data removed while keeping your account.
  4. Support must verify your identity and authority over the affected information, confirm the requested scope, and explain any effect on shared business history before removal.
  5. Ask for an acknowledgement, a reference for follow-up, the expected completion date and a final confirmation listing what was removed and what, if anything, remains.

Do not send passwords, sign-in or verification codes, access tokens, full card details, identity documents or additional copies of the dataset in your initial message. If support is unavailable or has not acknowledged the message, do not assume that deletion has started or completed.

Data covered by a request

A request may cover uploaded source files and datasets, saved manual entries and drafts, and associated processing records. Derived reports and metrics can depend on those records. Support must assess those dependencies and confirm whether related evidence, comparisons or reports also need removal or rebuilding; deleting an original file alone does not necessarily erase its derived information.

Data removal is distinct from closing your account or cancelling a subscription. A data-only request should leave your account and unrelated information in place. Tell support which business and records are in scope. Removal must not affect another business or information you do not have authority to remove.

Information that may be retained

  • Account and access information: your account profile and authentication records remain when you ask to keep your account.
  • Unrelated or shared business information: records outside the confirmed scope remain. Requests involving another person's or a shared business's records require appropriate authority.
  • Billing, security and legal records: some information may be needed for payment disputes, fraud prevention, security investigations or legal obligations. Support must identify any applicable exception rather than treating it as a blanket reason to retain requested datasets.
  • Support and provider records: the request conversation, email-delivery records, infrastructure logs and backups have separate retention settings. Relevant provider-held copies need to be considered as part of the request.
  • Exported copies: reports or files already saved on your device or shared with other recipients are not removed from those devices by a BIZNORYX request.

Deletion timing and retention periods

A fixed deletion deadline and specific backup and log expiry periods have not been confirmed for this publication. Support must confirm the applicable completion date, retention periods and reasons for any retained information after verifying the scope. Removing information from the active service does not immediately erase every backup, provider record or exported copy.

The request is not complete simply because support has acknowledged it. Ask for written confirmation of the removal, any outstanding provider or backup steps, and when any retained copies are expected to expire. You can follow up through the same support channel using the request reference.
